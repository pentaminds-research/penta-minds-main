Who We Are

Penta Minds is an independent AI research initiative evolving into an independent research collective focused on Artificial Intelligence, AI literacy, education, governance, responsible innovation, AI systems, and emerging technologies. It is not a temporary competition team, conventional student club, certification platform, generic technology community, or a startup focused solely on commercial AI applications. Instead, it is being developed as a student-led research ecosystem that builds continuity across research programmes, field studies, publications, educational resources, experiments, and collaborations.

Mission & Vision

To advance understanding of Artificial Intelligence through independent research, responsible experimentation, accessible knowledge, and collaborative innovation—while building the capabilities required for students and emerging researchers to meaningfully participate in the future of AI.

Research

Conduct evidence-based research into emerging AI technologies, systems, behaviours, applications, and societal implications.

Education

Make AI understandable and accessible through literacy, educational resources, research communication, and knowledge sharing.

Responsibility

Promote governance, ethics, safety, accountability, transparency, accessibility, and human-centred adoption.

Infrastructure

Build the organisational, digital, research, and collaborative foundations for long-term student-led AI research.

Our vision is to evolve into a credible, independent research institution contributing original knowledge, responsible innovation, and evidence-based perspectives to the future of Artificial Intelligence.

FLAGSHIP PROJECT

AI as the Hidden Curriculum

How Learning is Happening Through AI Without Being Taught

RolePrincipal Investigation
TimelineJuly – December 2026
StageActive Preparation and Foundation Phase
Output TypeResearch Report · Survey Dataset · AI Literacy Toolkit V2

AI as the Hidden Curriculum is the core research inquiry examining how students in Indian higher education are learning to use AI tools informally — outside any structured curriculum or institutional guidance. The central question: what does it mean when an entire generation of students is developing AI competency through trial, error, and peer observation, without the institution acknowledging it? This project generates the primary evidence base for the AI Grand Challenge submission.

Research Approach

1. Observe → 2. Question → 3. Investigate → 4. Analyse
5. Experiment → 6. Publish → 7. Share → 8. Iterate

Penta Minds follows a continuous cycle: Learn → Question → Research → Experiment → Build → Publish → Share → Collaborate → Scale. A student should not stop at “I learned AI”; the journey becomes “I learned it → questioned it → investigated it → tested it → built something → documented it → shared it → improved it.”
